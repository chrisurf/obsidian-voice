# Obsidian Voice 🔊

![Obsidian Voice — turn your vault into a personal audiobook](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chrisurf/obsidian-voice/main/assets/hero.png)

**Listen to your notes.** Voice reads your Obsidian notes aloud in natural, lifelike speech — with a player that feels like an audiobook app. Use the text-to-speech provider you already have: **OpenAI, ElevenLabs, AWS Polly, Google Cloud, Azure Speech, MiniMax** or any **OpenAI-compatible** server, even one running on your own computer.

## What you can do with it

- 🚶 **Listen on the go.** Turn notes, articles and book summaries into audio for your walk, commute or workout — and save them as MP3s to play offline.
- 📚 **Study by ear.** Make your course notes a playlist of chapters, replay the hard parts at your own speed, and bookmark what you want to review.
- ✍️ **Proofread your writing.** Hearing a draft read aloud reveals clumsy sentences and typos your eyes skip over.
- 🌍 **Practice a language.** Hear vocabulary and texts spoken by native-sounding voices in dozens of languages, slower or faster.
- 👀 **Rest your eyes.** Let Voice read long notes to you when your eyes are tired — or whenever reading is hard.
- 🎧 **Build a personal podcast.** Save readings into a folder and play them back to back, like the episodes of a show.

## How it works

1. **Connect a provider** — paste your API key and test it. [Pick a provider →](https://github.com/chrisurf/obsidian-voice/blob/main/docs/providers/README.md)
2. **Press play** on any note. Long notes start playing after the first part, while the rest is generated.
3. **Keep what you like** — save it as an MP3, bookmark the key moments, listen again any time.

## Highlights

**An audiobook player for your vault.** Play, pause, skip, change the speed from 0.5× to 2.0×, and see your saved audio as chapters you can play back to back. [The Voice player →](https://github.com/chrisurf/obsidian-voice/blob/main/docs/using-voice/player.md)

**Bookmarks that travel with the file.** Mark a spot with one tap and jump back later. Bookmarks are stored inside the MP3, so they also show up as chapters in podcast apps. [Bookmarks →](https://github.com/chrisurf/obsidian-voice/blob/main/docs/using-voice/bookmarks.md)

## Get started

1. In Obsidian, open **Settings → Community plugins → Browse**, search for **Voice**, install and enable it.
2. Open **Settings → Voice**, choose your provider, paste your key and select **Test Credentials**.
3. Open a note and press ▶️ in the Voice player.

The [User Guide](https://github.com/chrisurf/obsidian-voice/blob/main/docs/README.md) walks you through each step with pictures and covers every feature in depth.
